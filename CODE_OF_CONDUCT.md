# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and attention to the community's best interests. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this Code. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns through the private contact channel in the repository's security or community settings. If no such channel is available, use an established private channel to reach a maintainer. Do not publish sensitive details. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy and respond in proportion to the incident.
